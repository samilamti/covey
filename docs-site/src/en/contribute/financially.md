Covey is powered by volunteers and open source, but there are fixed costs that are necessary to keep the platform secure and accessible.

## What does the money go towards?

Your donations go directly to operating costs:

1. **BankID fees** -- Secure login and user verification involves transaction costs. This is our largest expense but critical to the app's function.
2. **Hosting and infrastructure** -- Servers, domain names and round-the-clock availability.
3. **Codeberg and tools** -- We support Codeberg as a platform and may need to pay for CI/CD services.

## How you can contribute

We are in the process of setting up formal donation channels. Soon you will be able to support us via:

- **Swish** -- Quick one-time donations.
- **Open Collective** -- Full transparency -- see exactly how every krona is used.
- **Codeberg / GitHub Sponsors** -- Monthly giving for long-term support.
