## Scenario: Walking home together

Many people feel unsafe walking home alone late at night, for example after a party or a cinema visit. Covey helps you find someone to walk with.

### Step by step

1. **Create a request** -- Open the app and choose "New request".
2. **Describe your route** -- Enter where you are starting from and where you are heading.
3. **Choose who can see the request** -- For added safety, you can limit visibility:
   - **Similar to me** -- Only people of the same sex and similar age (within 5 years).
   - **Qualified companions** -- The above plus people with a high safety score.
   - **All members** -- All verified users can see your request.

### Example

Emma is 19 and needs to walk home from a party. She opens Covey, creates a request and writes: "Walking from Hornsgatan towards Slussen in 10 minutes. Anyone want to join?" She sets the visibility so that only women her age can see the message. Before long, she gets a response from Sara who is on the same street.

## During the session

When someone accepts your request, an **active session** begins:

- **Real-time map** -- Both parties can see each other's position on the map.
- **Estimated time of arrival** -- Automatic calculation of approximate time until you meet.
- **Messaging** -- Chat directly in the app with quick messages like "I'm on my way" and "I can see you".
- **Mutual completion** -- Either party can mark the session as done. The other confirms.

## Benefits

- **Verified identity** -- Everyone has logged in with BankID. No anonymous users.
- **Privacy protection** -- Your exact location is only shared with the person helping you, and is automatically deleted when the session ends.
