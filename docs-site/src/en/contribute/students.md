Your curiosity and perspective are a great asset. Whether you study technology, social science or law, you can contribute.

## What can you do?

### Theses and papers

Use Covey as a case study in your studies:

- **Technology** -- Write about secure architecture for civic platforms or performance optimisation for mobile web.
- **Sociology** -- Investigate how digital tools can strengthen local cohesion and safety.
- **Design** -- Conduct user studies and suggest improvements to UX/UI.

### Spread the word

- Talk about the project at your university or student association.
- Help us reach younger audiences.

### Translation and documentation

- Help make the documentation clear and accessible.
- Translate the app into more languages to include more people.
