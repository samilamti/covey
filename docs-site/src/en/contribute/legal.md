Since we are building a platform for civic support with a focus on privacy and safety, there are many legal aspects where we need expertise.

## Areas

### GDPR and privacy

We take user privacy very seriously and never sell data.

- Help us ensure that we not only comply with the GDPR, but set a new standard for privacy protection.
- Review our processes for data minimisation and retention.

### User agreements and rules

- Draft clear and fair terms of service that protect both the platform and the individual.
- Formulate sustainable legal rules for the platform.

### Organisational form and licensing

- Advise on running non-profit associations or foundations.
- Ensure correct handling of open-source licences.
- Handle intellectual property matters.
