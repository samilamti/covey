You do not need to know how to code to make a difference. Your input is crucial for us to build something that works for everyone.

## Contribute in everyday life

### Beta test and give feedback

- Use the app in your everyday life.
- Report bugs or things that are confusing.
- Tell us what you are missing -- the best ideas come from real needs.

### Community and moderation

- Be a good role model in your local groups.
- Help welcome new users.
- Report inappropriate content to keep the platform safe.

### Spread the word

- Tell your neighbours, friends and family about Covey.
- Help older or less tech-savvy people around you get started.
- Share our posts on social media.
