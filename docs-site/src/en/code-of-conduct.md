To create a welcoming and effective environment, the following rules apply to everyone who participates in the Covey project and its community:

## 1. Respect

We maintain a respectful tone. No personal attacks or discrimination are tolerated. We support each other and treat one another with dignity.

## 2. Safety first

No code that compromises user privacy or safety will be accepted. Protecting our users is always our highest priority.

## 3. Transparency

All decisions are made openly. Our source code is open, our discussions are public and our finances are transparent.

## 4. Quality

We prioritise performance and resource efficiency to include users with older hardware. Covey should work for everyone, not just those with the latest technology.

## 5. Licensing

All contributions are made under the project's open-source licence. By contributing, you agree that your work is shared under the same terms.
